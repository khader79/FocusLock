# FocusLock – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-26

FocusLock collects **no personal data**. There are no accounts, no analytics,
no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs.

## What the app does on your device

- Words you type in search fields and browser address bars are checked
  **in memory, on your device**, against an offline blocklist. Nothing typed
  is saved, uploaded, or shared.
- The blocked-attempts log lives only in the running app and disappears when
  the app closes.
- The support message shown on bypass attempts is stored as static app text.

## Network filtering

Adult-site blocking at the network level is performed by **Android's Private
DNS setting and your chosen DNS provider** (e.g. Cloudflare Family), not by
this app. This app sends no network requests of its own.

## Permissions used and why

- **Accessibility Service** – reads search/address-field text to detect
  blocked words and returns you to the Home screen on a match. Required for
  the core blocking feature; shown with an explicit disclosure before you
  enable it.
- **Notifications** – shows the personal support message when a bypass is
  attempted. Optional; the app works without it.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the Play Store
